Your rides. Your runs.
Your health.

Singletrack Health is a fitness tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch built for cyclists, runners, hikers, and walkers who want a simple, private way to record activities and track progress over time.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone & Apple Watch
🗺️

GPS activity tracking

Record cycling, running, walking, hiking, and indoor runs with real-time GPS route maps, live pace, distance, elevation, and heart rate — right on your wrist or phone.

⌚

Apple Watch companion

Start, pause, and finish workouts directly from your watch. Rides sync automatically to your iPhone the moment you open the app, with no manual transfers needed.

📊

Stats & yearly goals

Set distance goals for each activity type and track your progress throughout the year. Get notified at 80%, 90%, and 100% so you never lose sight of your target.

🏆

Personal records & achievements

Automatically surface your longest ride, fastest pace, most elevation, and more — per activity type. Unlock badges for milestones like century rides, marathons, and 30-day streaks.

🔥

Activity streaks

Stay motivated with a running streak counter that tracks consecutive active days. See your current streak, all-time best, and personalized encouragement to keep it going.

👥

Social feed

Follow friends, share activities, and give kudos. Find people you know via username search or QR code. Your social data stays in CloudKit — no third-party social networks.

🌡️

Ambient weather

See the temperature at the time and place of each outdoor activity, automatically fetched after your ride using open weather data — no account required.

📁

GPX import & export

Import GPX files from other devices and export any activity to share routes or move data wherever you need it.

🔲

Widgets & complications

Glance at today's miles, weekly totals, streak, and yearly goal progress from your Home Screen or watch face — no need to open the app.

Private by design. Your workout data lives in your private iCloud account via Apple CloudKit and syncs across your devices automatically. Singletrack Health does not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or store it on third-party servers. Health data accessed through Apple HealthKit never leaves your device ecosystem.
